## Rules used for the decisions

- R1. Read the rubric in window.FTM_DATA.dimensions literally. Where it is silent, follow the precedent of the 41 profiles already checked.
- R2. Legal 3: a personal penalty, even settled without admission (precedents: Dell, Bechtolsheim), or a personal indictment even if later dropped (Adani). A French mise en examen is the formal charging step by an investigating judge and counts as an indictment.
- R3. A personal settlement with no penalty, including a tax settlement, is legal 2, following the Simons precedent. The rubric does not cover this case; see the note on rubric gaps.
- R4. A personal penalty set aside on appeal is not a standing penalty. The rubric's 'even if later dropped' covers indictments only, so it does not reach legal 3. The matter stays in the person's legal record.
- R5. Legal 2 at company level needs a major outcome: a court decision establishing wrongdoing in a contested case, a corporate plea, court administration, or a settlement above $1 billion that pays for alleged wrongdoing. A ruling on the validity of a contract, a defamation suit the company itself lost, a settlement paid mostly in share purchases, and a small or old fine upheld on appeal stay at 1.
- R6. Unresolved 3: the person is a named defendant in a pending case.
- R7. Politics 3: a personal role in the institutions of an authoritarian state, or an official listing (precedents: Mordashov, Mikhelson). Company-level ties alone are politics 2.
- R8. Counterweights need giving or verified results by the person, their company or their foundation. Company figures are kept when labelled as such. A text about money raised from others, or grants only committed by a charity the person does not fund, is dropped.

Rubric gaps to settle:

- Personal civil settlements with no penalty (Simons, Pignataro, Ellison 2005) fall between levels 2 and 3. All three are scored 2 here; the rubric should say so.
- Personal penalties set aside on appeal (Ambani) are treated differently from dropped indictments (Adani, Bettencourt Meyers). The rubric should say whether that is intended.
- Most proposed increases are on dimensions whose current value was a default 0 ('No record checked yet'). Of the 41 profiles already checked, 13 still have such a default on legal, so totals are not yet comparable across all checked profiles. A refresh pass on those defaults is the next step.
